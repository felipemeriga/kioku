01

Overview

● pinned

Cosm’s Rust workspace for producing low-latency 360° sports video and operating the Sports Event Service (SES).

The lead player fetches tiled TiledMedia feeds, decodes left/right subgrids with NVDEC, stitches and dewarps with CUDA, encodes with NVENC, and sends GStreamer output to OME/SRT or MP4. SES ingests, stores, and serves sports tracking used for player targeting and camera selection.

The workspace also includes GPU/TiledMedia wrappers, an on-prem management tool, player UI, regression tooling, and deployment automation.

02

Architecture

● pinned

lead/src/main.rs enters cli/config, then player::main. A player loop owns playback, selection and control; two SideWorkers own left/right TiledMedia cursors and NVDEC caches; a same-index frame gate feeds CUDA stitch/dewarp and NVENC; an async output worker passes zero-copy encoded outcomes through GStreamer. SES is a parallel ingestion/Mongo/gRPC subsystem supplying tracking data.
